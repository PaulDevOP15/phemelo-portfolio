A little about me

About me

I’m Phemelo Makaota, a junior full-stack web developer based in Pretoria, South Africa. I use JavaScript, React, Node.js, Express, MongoDB and Firebase to build web applications, connecting responsive interfaces with APIs, databases and authentication.

My project experience includes e-commerce, appointment booking and workflow automation, with features such as shopping carts, payment integration, availability checks and email notifications. I enjoy solving practical problems and understanding how each part of an application works together. I’m seeking a junior developer role where I can contribute and develop my skills, and I’m also available for freelance website projects.
